Chapter 3 — The bridge

The rain had stopped by the time she reached the bridge, and the river below was the colour of old pewter.

She had told him she would not come. She had told herself the same thing, twice, on the stairs.

“You came,” he said, which was not a question, and she did not answer it as one.

The lamps along the parapet came on one by one, as if someone were counting.
